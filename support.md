# Tempo Support

Thanks for using Tempo – ADHD Daily Planner.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?**
Open Tempo, tap any Pro feature to open the Tempo Pro screen, then tap **Restore**. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

**How do I cancel?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, then **Tempo**. You can also tap **Manage subscription** on Tempo's Progress tab. If you cancel during the 7-day free trial you will not be charged.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Where is my data stored?**
On your phone only. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
