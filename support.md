# Tempo Support

Thanks for using Tempo – ADHD Daily Planner.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?**
Open Tempo, tap any Pro feature to open the Tempo Pro screen, then tap **Restore**. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you subscribed with.

**How do I cancel?**
On iPhone, subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, then **Tempo**. On Android, they are managed by Google Play: open the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions**, **Subscriptions**, then **Tempo**. On either phone you can also tap **Manage subscription** on Tempo's Progress tab. If you cancel during the 7-day free trial you will not be charged.

**How do I get a refund?**
On iPhone, refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). On Android, they are handled by Google Play: see [Google Play's refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

**Where is my data stored?**
On your phone only. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md) and [terms of use](terms.md).
