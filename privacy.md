# Tempo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Tempo – ADHD Daily Planner ("Tempo", "we") is a planner and focus timer for iPhone. This policy explains what data Tempo handles and why. The short version: your tasks and focus history stay on your phone, we do not sell data, show ads or track you across apps.

## Data that stays on your device

Your tasks, focus sessions, streaks and the answers you give in the welcome quiz are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders (the daily check-in and the "focus block done" alert) are scheduled locally on your device. Tempo does not use push notification servers.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To unlock Tempo Pro and restore purchases, Tempo uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created for your install, your purchase and subscription history from the App Store, and basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, country and language. This data is used only to provide and support your subscription. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, health data or browsing history.
- We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
- We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Children

Tempo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Your choices and rights

Because your planner data lives on your device, you control it: delete tasks in the app or delete the app to erase it. To ask about or delete the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat, contact us below and we will handle the request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
