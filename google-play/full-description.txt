Big to-do lists freeze ADHD brains. Tempo shrinks your day into small, timeboxed steps and keeps you company while you do them.

PLAN IN TINY TIMEBOXES
Write the smallest next step, give it 5, 15, 25 or 45 minutes, and you're done planning. No projects, no tags, no overwhelm.

FOCUS WITH A BUDDY
Start a focus block and Tempo stays with you, with gentle check-ins like "Just this one thing" and "Drifted? No problem, come back." You get a nudge when the block ends, even if you wandered off to another app.

A PLAN BUILT AROUND YOU
A one-minute quiz learns what trips you up and when your brain works best, then sets up your daily check-in at that time.

STREAKS THAT DON'T PUNISH YOU
Finish one task or one focus block to keep your streak alive. Missed a day? Tempo Pro's weekly streak freeze has your back.

YOUR MONTH AT A GLANCE (TEMPO PRO)
The calendar shows your timeboxes, streak days and journal check-ins. Tap any day to see what happened, or plan timeboxes for the days ahead.

A TWO-LINE JOURNAL (TEMPO PRO)
Pick a mood, jot a line or two, done. Over time you'll see what helps you focus and what gets in the way.

SEE WHAT WORKS
Weekly insights show how much you focused each day, so you can plan around your good days.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your tasks and focus history stay on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
